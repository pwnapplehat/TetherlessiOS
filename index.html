Everything on-device

Your iPhone is the whole toolchain.

No desktop app running in the background, no USB cable, no weekly trip to a computer. Tetherless signs, installs, and keeps your apps alive entirely on the phone.

Sign & install any IPA

Bring an .ipa and install it in a few taps. Use your free Apple ID, or bring your own certificate — Tetherless handles the signing for you.

  • Free Apple ID signing, or your own paid certificate
  • Organized library: Unsigned → Signed → Installed
  • One-tap resign to keep apps alive past 7 days
1Import your IPAFrom Files, AirDrop, or a download
2Sign itApple ID or your certificate
3Installed on your iPhoneStraight to the Home Screen

Get a clean copy of apps you own

Tetherless can download an app you already own from the App Store and produce a DRM-free copy — fully on-device, no Mac and no jailbreak required.

  • Search, download, and decrypt in one place
  • Pick a specific version, or the latest
  • Saved straight into your library, ready to sign

Get started

Up and running in four steps.

STEP 1

Download & install

Grab the build that matches your iOS version and put Tetherless on your iPhone.

STEP 2

Pair your iPhone

A quick one-time setup lets your iPhone talk to itself — or bring your own certificate and skip it.

STEP 3

Add an app

Import an IPA, or download a clean copy of one you own right inside Tetherless.

STEP 4

Sign & install

Tap sign, tap install, and it lands on your Home Screen. Resign anytime to keep it.

Download Legacy

On iPhone running iOS 17.4 or later. Free Apple-ID signing uses the free LocalDevVPN app from the App Store for the on-device connection; custom-certificate mode needs neither pairing nor a VPN. On-device “Pair with…” needs iOS 27 — on earlier versions you import a pairing file instead.
